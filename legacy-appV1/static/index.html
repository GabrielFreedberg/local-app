Local first-cut of the grocery + company deal workflow. Users log in with email and password, save their alert interests, and only get alerts for matching deals in their zip code.

Email delivery uses SMTP when configured. Without SMTP settings, sends appear in notification_log.jsonl as mocked notifications.
